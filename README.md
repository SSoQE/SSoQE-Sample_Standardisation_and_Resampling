## 🌿 About the lesson

This lesson covers approaches for making ecological samples comparable, including rarefaction, bootstrapping, and coverage-based reasoning.

## **🎯 Learning goals**

For this session, participants learn to:

- Understand how sampling effort and spatial dependence bias ecological data;

<!-- -->

-  Apply rarefaction, coverage-based standardisation, bootstrapping, and spatial thinning;

<!-- -->

-  Quantify uncertainty arising from incomplete sampling;

<!-- -->

-  Critically assess when standardisation is useful and when modelling the sampling process is preferable.
